Dog Stories Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Dog Stories is a cozy merge-and-story game. This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: we don't collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Dog Stories has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. That includes the items on your board, your score and level, which stories you've read, your requests, your energy and your sound settings. This information never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

The Unity game engine

Dog Stories is built with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. Depending on how the app is built, the engine may send Unity a small amount of anonymous technical information, such as device model, operating system version and screen size, to help improve the engine. It doesn't include your name, contact details or anything you do in the game. Unity's privacy policy is at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Children's privacy

Dog Stories is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information, it doesn't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@sillywillyapps.com.
