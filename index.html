Dog Stories: Cozy Merge

Merge toys, unlock dog tales.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Dog Stories is a cozy merge game about dogs and the people who love them. Tap or drag three matching items to merge bones, bowls, balls, toys and beds into ever-fancier treasures, and unlock beautifully illustrated stories along the way.

Meet Max, the shy Golden Retriever finding his forever home; Bella, the Border Collie who keeps escaping the yard; Daisy, the Rat Terrier who meets a very surprising "cat"; Charlie, the Beagle whose nose saves the day; Luna, the clumsy German Shepherd who becomes a therapy dog; and Rocky, the little French Bulldog with the biggest heart in the park.

  • Five merge chains, each with five tiers
  • Illustrated story chapters with choices and gentle lessons
  • Requests from each dog's human, with rewards
  • No accounts, no ads, plays offline
