New

Meet Coco, and groom her yourself

Coco is an apricot standard poodle, groomed to perfection for the town dog show, right up until she spots a puddle. Her three-chapter story follows her and her human, Marisol, at the grooming salon on Main Street, and ends with a charity dog wash where every dog from the other stories lines up.

After the puddle, it's your turn at the grooming table: scrub off the mud with a soapy sponge, move the dryer over her until she's fluffy again, then drag a pink bow onto her topknot. There's no timer and no way to fail, and each step gives a bit of bonus energy the first time. Look for Coco from level 25.

This update also brings new chapters for Biscuit, Noodle and Waffles: a surprise birthday party on Maple Street, the Dachshund Dash, and the biggest bath Marisol's salon has ever seen.

New

Meet Calvin and Pickles

Calvin is a fluffy little cream Pomeranian whose Saturday game of fetch takes a turn when he spots a cat stuck up an oak tree. His three-chapter story comes with three new park games, with yaps and meows to match:

  • Help Pickles down! Press and hold to keep Calvin calm so the cat can climb down the oak's trunk, a little at a time. Let go and he starts yapping.
  • Dog-and-cat fetch. Flick the ball across the park and watch Calvin and Pickles take turns chasing it.
  • Catch the ball! Pickles knocks the ball out of the tree, and you slide Calvin under its shadow to catch it.

Like the other mini-games, there's no timer and no way to fail, and each one gives a bit of bonus energy the first time you play it. Look for Calvin from level 22.

New

Pick your colors

Dog Stories now comes in three color themes: the original warm Default, a soft purple Lavender and a gentle pink Rose. Tap the gear button to open Settings and tap a theme. The whole game changes at once, and it remembers your pick next time.

New

Kiss it better with Waffles

Waffles is an enormous English Mastiff with a very small scrape and a very big opinion about it. Help his human, Priya, in a sweet new mini-game: rub the paw clean with a soft cloth, drag the bandage into place and smooth it down, then press and hold to give a kiss. Mwah!

There's no timer and no way to get it wrong, and each step gives a bit of bonus energy the first time you finish it. Waffles' story also brings a new family of items to merge, from a Small Bandage all the way up to the Legendary Dog Cone. Look for him at level 19.

New

Sniff it out with Charlie

Some story chapters now open a little hidden-object game. Drag a sunny meadow under Charlie's nose and watch the sniff meter warm up from "Faint…" to "Right here!" as you get close. Hidden treasures pop up right on his nose: tap one to collect it.

There's no timer and no way to fail, so take your time. Each treasure gives a bit of bonus energy the first time you find it. Look for it in Charlie's "Training Day" chapter.

Dog Stories is a cozy merge game about dogs and the people who love them. Tap or drag three matching items to merge bones, bowls, balls, toys and beds into ever-fancier treasures, and unlock beautifully illustrated stories along the way.

Meet Max, the shy Golden Retriever finding his forever home; Bella, the Border Collie who keeps escaping the yard; Daisy, the Rat Terrier who meets a very surprising "cat"; Charlie, the Beagle whose nose saves the day; Luna, the clumsy German Shepherd who becomes a therapy dog; Rocky, the little French Bulldog with the biggest heart in the park; and Waffles, the enormous English Mastiff who makes a very big fuss about a very small scrape.

  • Five merge chains, each with five tiers
  • Illustrated story chapters with choices and gentle lessons
  • Requests from each dog's human, with rewards
  • Cozy mini-games inside some stories: sniff out treasures, patch up a paw, play in the park with Calvin and Pickles, or groom Coco the poodle
  • Three color themes: Default, Lavender and Rose
  • Scoop up the occasional mess, and trade Legendary items for energy and points
  • No accounts, no ads, plays offline
