Dog Stories Support

Thanks for playing Dog Stories! If something isn't working or you have an idea, email support@sillywillyapps.com and include your device model and what happened.

Common questions

How do I merge?
Tap three matching items (the same item at the same tier), or drag items so that three matching ones touch. They merge into the next tier.
What does Fetch do?
Fetch brings a new item onto the board for 1 energy. Energy refills over time, even while the game is closed.
The board is full. What now?
Merge some items to make room. Every merge frees up space.
How do I unlock more stories?
Stories unlock as you level up. The banner above the board shows the level for the next one, and completing requests earns extra points.
What is the bonus clock on a request?
Finish a request before its clock runs out for +15 bonus energy. The clock only counts time you're playing, so it stops during stories, in the Stories book and while the game is closed. If it runs out, nothing is lost: you can still finish the request and get the normal reward.
What is the daily request?
An optional extra shown below the board, with a new one each day. Finish it for +20 energy and 100 points. Tap it to see what it asks for.
Can I read a story again?
Yes. Tap the book button next to the request banner and choose Read again.
How do I turn the music or sounds off?
Tap the book button, then use the Music and Sounds toggles at the bottom.
Will I lose my progress?
Your progress is saved on your device automatically. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
