Dog Stories Support

Thanks for playing Dog Stories! If something isn't working or you have an idea, email support@sillywillyapps.com and include your device model and what happened.

Common questions

How do I merge?
Tap three matching items (the same item at the same tier), or drag items so that three matching ones touch. They merge into the next tier. See How to play for a picture guide.
What does Fetch do?
Fetch is a power-up: it brings a new item onto the board for 1 energy. Every merge also uses 1 energy, but moving items around the board is free. You have up to 50, and 1 comes back every 2 minutes, even while the game is closed. When it runs out, the dogs take a nap so you can take a break.
What is +50 Energy?
Tap the energy bar to see when your next energy arrives. If you don't want to wait, +50 Energy is an in-app purchase that adds 50 energy straight away, even above the usual 50. A grown-up has to answer a quick question first, and then the App Store or Google Play asks to confirm the purchase. Bought energy is kept until you use it. Refunds go through the store you bought from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google Play at Google Play Help.
What do I do about the dog poop?
Sometimes a free item turns out to be a little mess instead. It can't be moved or merged. Tap it to clean it up with your pooper scooper and poop bag, which uses 1 energy. A Fetch always brings a real item, never a mess.
What are Legendary items for?
Legendary items are the top of each family and don't merge any further. Tap one to trade it in for 5 energy and 500 points.
Where do bandages come from?
Bandages join the regular drops once you finish Waffles' story at level 19. Three Large Bandages make Gauze, and three Gauze make the Legendary Dog Cone.
Can I fail the paw-care game, Calvin's park games or Coco's grooming game?
No. There's no timer and no way to get it wrong. You can skip it from the story, or tap Done to leave at any time.
The board is full. What now?
Merge some items to make room. Every merge frees up space.
How do I unlock more stories?
Stories unlock as you level up. The banner above the board shows the level for the next one, and completing requests earns extra points.
What is the bonus clock on a request?
Finish a request before its clock runs out for +5 bonus energy. The clock only counts time you're playing, so it stops during stories, in the Stories book, in Settings and while the game is closed. If it runs out, nothing is lost: you can still finish the request and get the normal reward.
What is the daily request?
An optional extra shown below the board, with a new one each day. Finish it for +5 energy and 100 points. Tap it to see what it asks for.
Can I read a story again?
Yes. Tap the book button next to the request banner and choose Read again.
How do I turn the music or sounds off?
Tap the gear button at the top right, then use the Music and Sounds toggles.
Can I choose the music?
Yes. Tap the gear button, then tap Style to switch between Shuffle all, Chill, Piano, Chiptune, Jazz & Funk, Puzzle and Mellow. Next song skips ahead, and Music credits lists every track and who made it.
Will I lose my progress?
Your progress is saved on your device automatically. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
